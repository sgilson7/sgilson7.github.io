Teaching

When I teach, I try to make the reasoning visible. Before we choose a formula or a rule, I want us to identify what the problem asks and why that method applies. When an answer goes wrong, we can often learn something useful by finding the first step that no longer matches the problem.

That approach also shapes my AI literacy instruction. I ask students to examine a chatbot’s reasoning, test examples, and explain the solution themselves. The course materials include the assignments and procedures used in an ongoing discrete mathematics study.
