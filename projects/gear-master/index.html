Game

Gear Master

Build gear out of parts, then watch your character auto-battle. Rust + macroquad.

Written in
Rust
Run it
https://sgilson7.github.io/gear-master/
Source
https://github.com/sgilson7/gear-master

You build a character out of parts, and then you watch them fight without you.

Components form items when their shapes touch and satisfy a recipe. Items activate on cooldowns, and the battle runs itself. How you packed the grid is the whole of your input, which means every fight is a verdict on a decision you made before it started.

Written in Rust with macroquad.
