Ribbit King

Game

Ribbit King is a 3D game of frolf, golf played with frogs, that runs in a browser. It is a fan game after Bandai's Ribbit King for the GameCube (2003), built from a design a friend wrote, and it uses none of Bandai's material. Each frog sits on a catapult like a carnival strength tester. You whack it with a mallet, then fly the frog through the air, and the fewest strokes into the lotus flower at the end of the hole wins.

The swing is a timing meter. A needle runs round a circle with a red power bar on it, and the gold notch in the middle of the bar is a perfect shot: full power, dead straight and a little extra. Elsewhere on the bar the shot is weaker, and an early hit hooks left while a late one slices right. Off the bar is a whiff. Before the swing you aim, set the mallet's power and pick a loft, and a dotted arc shows where a perfect hit would first land.

In the air you steer the frog. Power-ups give an extra jump, wings that glide like Mario 64's wing cap, and explosive mushrooms that blast the frog away. Lily pads and spider webs throw it back up. Lines of flies, after the flies in The Binding of Isaac, hang over each hole like Sonic's rings. Catch ten and your tongue can knock another frog flying. Water, lava and out of bounds each cost a stroke. The four holes are a swamp, a desert oasis, a city street with traffic, and a lava flow under a volcano. Up to four frogs, people or computer players, take turns from the same tee, and the leader wears a crown.

Every rule runs in a Rust core in fixed-point integer maths, compiled to WebAssembly, and the page draws what the core returns. The flight the player steers, the aiming arc and the computer players' imagined shots all run on the same physics step. The menu art and the frog portraits are drawn in TikZ.

Playing it

A and D aim, W and S set the power, and Q changes the loft. Space starts the meter, and Space again whacks. In the air, W, A, S and D steer and Space jumps. E uses the tongue once you have ten flies. Drag with the mouse to look around. Escape pauses. Your best scores are kept in the browser you played in.
