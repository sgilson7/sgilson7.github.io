Choomstar

Game

Choomstar is a single-player first-person movement shooter for a desktop browser, built on Chumontillado's core with its movement, weapons, maps and skins unchanged. The player is Long Time Comin's diver in a pressure suit, and the look is chrome and space age.

Missions are the game. Fifty of them sit on a star chart of eight tiers, from Launch to Choomstar, and each is a short challenge with bronze, silver and gold medals; a mission opens when the medals it asks for are earned. There are eleven kinds: break targets, run parkour courses against the clock, fight with one fixed weapon, last out hordes of zombies that hurt anyone within reach, fight bots alone or in teams, fight with melee weapons only, carry relics to the lift in salvage, clear arenas where Long Time Comin's foes fire their orb patterns, bring down bosses whose phases change their attacks, and run gauntlets of fights in a row on one health bar. The chart and a scrolling mission list show every mission, and a locked one says what opens it. Medals and best times are kept in the browser. The practice room and a match against bots are there too.

It is a Builder project: a Rust core holds every rule, a WebAssembly shim passes values to the page, and the page only draws what the core returns. The missions are data, so a new pack is a new file.

Playing it

Move with WASD and look with the mouse; space jumps, and Shift or C crouches. The left button fires or swings, and the right button is a weapon's second action. 1 to 0 take a gun, Q the last one again, E a melee weapon and R reloads. Esc pauses and lists the controls. The game captures the mouse, so it plays on a desktop browser.

Checked in Chromium, Firefox and WebKit.
