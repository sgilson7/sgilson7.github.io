Tools and games

These projects grew out of research tasks and game development. The research tools help with assignment testing and PDF review; the games explore browser-based simulation and equipment-building systems.

Classroom tools

These help a teacher run and read a lesson. Each one computes everything on the device that opens it, and each ships with sample content meant to show the tool rather than to be taught. Using these tools in a school says what each tool sends, what it keeps in the browser, and what a school device has to allow.

  • BT Lab

    A classroom demo of how a behavior tree becomes key presses, one tick at a time, using the opponents from Vagrancy.

    Visit tool: BT LabRead more about BT Lab

  • Packet Alley

    Watch packets move on a network and solve TCP/IP challenges: address a packet, swap a hub for a switch, fix a route, open a connection.

    Visit tool: Packet AlleyRead more about Packet Alley

  • Circus Math Lab

    Three math-circle toys inside a circus game: plot a function by bouncing, integrate a flight with popcorn, and check a juggling pattern.

    Visit tool: Circus Math LabRead more about Circus Math Lab

  • Practice Results

    Students practise a question set and save a result file; the teacher opens the files together to see which topics to reteach.

    Visit tool: Practice ResultsRead more about Practice Results

  • Warm-ups and Exit Tickets

    Build a warm-up or an exit ticket from a question bank, chosen by source document and Depth of Knowledge level.

    Visit tool: Warm-ups and Exit TicketsRead more about Warm-ups and Exit Tickets

  • Feedback Drafts

    Pick a level for each criterion of your own rubric and get a feedback draft to edit and copy, built from the comments you wrote.

    Visit tool: Feedback DraftsRead more about Feedback Drafts

  • Research Discovery Lab

    A follow-along lab for setting up research profiles, author records, crawling checks, and discovery tests.

    Visit tool: Research Discovery LabRead more about Research Discovery Lab

  • Status Trends

    Open a tracker or gradebook export and see items per year, quarter or month by status, the change between periods, and the risks, all inside the browser tab.

    Visit tool: Status TrendsRead more about Status Trends

  • Formula Builder

    Mark the concepts in a formula and make it something students can hover, drag and step through, then export it as a PNG, SVG, GIF, LaTeX or an embed for another page.

    Visit tool: Formula BuilderRead more about Formula Builder

With collaborators

Packet Alley, listed above under classroom tools, was co-designed with a lab colleague who teaches TCP/IP and asked for a game that shows packets moving on a network.

Peter (ptonkle) makes browser games with Builder. In his own words:

  • Ape Race: 3D jungle vine-swinging race in the browser · momentum, items, six apes · against the AI or online for up to four
  • Mandible: Ant-colony real-time strategy in the browser · 1v1, 2v2 and Free-for-all · against the AI or online
  • Honey Hole: Cartoon fishing on a foggy lake · hook a monster, then hang on while your rivals come to ram you · against the AI or online
  • Crabble: A word game for crabs · scuttle, snatch letters and steal each other's words · against the AI or online
