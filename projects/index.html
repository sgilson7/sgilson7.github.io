Tools and games

These projects grew out of research tasks and game development. The research tools help with assignment testing and PDF review; the games explore browser-based simulation and equipment-building systems.

Research tools

  • Perturbation Workbench

    Record questions, rubrics, and chatbot attempts during iterative assignment perturbation.

  • PDF Redactor

    Find identifiers, review redaction boxes, and export a rebuilt PDF in your browser.

  • Builder

    A template, agent instructions and one check command for building small browser tools with a coding agent, with every rule in a tested Rust core.

Classroom tools

These help a teacher run and read a lesson. Each one computes everything on the device that opens it, and each ships with sample content meant to show the tool rather than to be taught.

  • Practice Results

    Students practise a question set and save a result file; the teacher opens the files together to see which topics to reteach.

  • Warm-ups and Exit Tickets

    Build a warm-up or an exit ticket from a question bank, chosen by source document and Depth of Knowledge level.

  • Feedback Drafts

    Pick a level for each criterion of your own rubric and get a feedback draft to edit and copy, built from the comments you wrote.

  • Circus Math Lab

    Three math-circle toys inside a circus game: plot a function by bouncing, integrate a flight with popcorn, and check a juggling pattern.

  • Research Discovery Lab

    A follow-along lab for setting up research profiles, author records, crawling checks, and discovery tests.

  • BT Lab

    A classroom demo of how a behavior tree becomes key presses, one tick at a time, using the opponents from Vagrancy.

Games

  • Vagrancy

    ★ Featured

    A two-player physics sword-fighting game in which each player controls only their fighter's arms.

  • Gear Master

    Arrange components into gear, then watch the build take part in an automatic battle.

  • Gear Master 2D

    Explore a tile-based world and assemble equipment for automatic battles.

  • Floodline

    A multiplayer medieval city builder in which each age ends in a flood.

  • Food Truck Circus

    A 3D trampoline game on a food truck, played across the real venues of a touring circus.

  • Long Time Comin'

    A third-person bullet-hell roguelike: sent back in time to stop yourself, fifty years too early, across a city and then the ice.

  • Pursuer

    A first-person survival driving game: hold on to something too long and a giant flat cut-out chases you through a city it tears apart.

  • Iron Vector

    A single-player 3D mech game: build a frame from parts in the garage, then fly it across a city.

  • Slushline

    A game in which a belt carries cups under slush spouts, and each spout pours for as long as you hold its key.
