Tools and games

Research tools

The Perturbation Workbench runs the protocol from the AIED 2026 paper; PDF Redactor de-identifies the student work a study collects. Both run inside your browser tab. You don't sign in, nothing is uploaded, and once the page has loaded it stops talking to the internet — open the Network tab and watch.

  • Perturbation Workbench

    Run the AIED 2026 iterative perturbation protocol in the browser. Nothing is uploaded.

    RustOpen the tool Source
  • PDF Redactor

    Redact student names and strip all metadata from PDFs, entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

    RustOpen the tool Source

Games

These are games. They are also where I work out engine ideas that turn up later in the research tools. All three run in a browser.

  • Gear Master

    Build gear out of parts, then watch your character auto-battle. Rust + macroquad.

    RustPlay Source
  • Gear Master 2D

    A 2D tile-based open-world RPG on the gear-assembly auto-battler from gear-master.

    RustPlay Source
  • Floodline

    Multiplayer medieval city builder in the browser. Deterministic lockstep over WebRTC; every age ends in a flood.

    RustPlay Source
