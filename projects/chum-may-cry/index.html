CHUM MAY CRY

Game

CHUM MAY CRY is a third-person sci-fi hack and slash for the browser: combat after Devil May Cry 5 and Dragon's Dogma 2, characters built from primitives, bright plant-like fauna, and a park on a hill above a lake with the colony city across the water. The story's arenas have tutorials that teach by play and checkpoints just before each fight, and a sandbox lets you spawn enemies and fight them.

Every rule lives in a Rust core compiled to WebAssembly; the page draws what the core returns. Playtester agents tuned the feel with a harness that plays scripted fights and prints what each tick did. It is built with Builder.

Playing it

Play with keyboard and mouse: WASD, left click light, right click heavy, E launcher, Space jump, hold Q with left click for the Whirlwind Strike, Tab lock-on, Shift sprint. Or plug in a PlayStation or Xbox controller: Square light, Triangle heavy, Circle launcher, Cross jump, L1 with Square for the Whirlwind Strike, R1 lock-on. Hits rumble the pad where the browser allows it.

Checked in Chromium, Firefox and WebKit.
