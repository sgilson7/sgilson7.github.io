Gear Master 2D

Game

Gear Master 2D combines equipment assembly with exploration. You travel through a tile-based world, encounter creatures, visit towns, and prepare a build for automatic combat.

Components go onto equipment grids, where their arrangement determines which item recipes are completed. That gives exploration and preparation a shared purpose: finding parts and deciding how to use them.

It builds on Gear Master, but you can start here without having played the earlier game.
