Concept

One-Shot GenAI Resistance

One-Shot GenAI Resistance is a property of an assignment question: a target chatbot scores below 60% on it across three separate attempts with the same prompt.

Also written: One-Shot Generative AI Resistance · One-Shot GenAI-Resistant · One-Shot GenAI Resistant · AI-resistant assignment

Defined in
Adversarial Assignment Perturbation: Effects on Help-Seeking Behaviors in Student Generative AI Chatbot Use, Section 2 and Table 1
DOI
https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-29770-9_48
Tool
Perturbation Workbench

The threshold is 60%, and it was not chosen for neatness. The paper grounds it at the point where a student in the United States typically stops receiving course credit, so a question that holds a model below it is a question a student cannot outsource and still pass.

Resistance is a property of a question against a named model at a named time, not a permanent quality. The AIED 2026 study tested against Google Gemini 2.0-flash, the free tier available to students through the university during Summer 2025. A question resistant to that model may not be resistant to the next one, which is why the protocol records which model it was run against.

One attempt is not enough to claim it. A model that fails once may pass on a re-roll, so the classification requires three failures of the exact same query against fresh instances.
