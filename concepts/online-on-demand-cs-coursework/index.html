Concept

online, on-demand CS coursework

Online, on-demand CS coursework is computing coursework a student takes outside a degree programme, on their own schedule, to supplement that programme.

Also written: OOD CS coursework · OODCS · on-demand computer science coursework

Defined in
How Do Undergraduates Interested in Computer Science Use Online, On-Demand CS Coursework?, Abstract and Section 2.2
DOI
https://doi.org/10.1145/3698205.3733938

Students supplement a degree programme with coursework they find themselves: platform courses, video series, free trials. This study asked what they get out of it and why they stop.

The L@S 2025 study surveyed 51 undergraduates enrolled in or interested in computer science, and interviewed four of them.

Across those 51 respondents, students reported using this coursework primarily to prepare for interviews and to build skills for their resumes, and perceived it positively. In the same survey of 51, the study found a positive correlation between students’ self-perceptions of learning during the coursework and their intention to persist with it — a correlation, and the study measured nothing about cause.

The main reason students gave for stopping was the loss of trial access, and replacement by large language models such as ChatGPT.
