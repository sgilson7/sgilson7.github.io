Concept

Free-Tier Accessible Model

A Free-Tier Accessible Model is one a student can reach through a consumer chatbot interface without a paid subscription.

Five models across the three major consumer chatbot products, verified as free-tier accessible in April 2026. The tier moves, so the list is dated rather than permanent.

Also written: free-tier model · free-tier chatbot

Introduced in
Sam Gilson. Free-tier Large Language Models on the MATH Benchmark: Implications for AI Literacy Instruction. AERA 2027 annual meeting.
Described in
Gilson, 2026 · Free-tier Large Language Models on the MATH Benchmark: Implications for AI Literacy Instruction, Methods; Data Sources and Materials

Research on what chatbots can do usually evaluates the paid tier. Students mostly use the free one.

That gap matters because the free tier is not a slightly worse version of the same thing; it is a different set of models with different weaknesses. The five evaluated here were reachable through an ordinary consumer chatbot interface, with no subscription, across the three major products, as of April 2026. The tier moves, which is why that date is attached to the list rather than left off.

Nearby terms in the literature: model capability evaluation, digital divide. None of these is a synonym for Free-Tier Accessible Model; they name related work this paper did not measure against.

Related terms

  • Calibrated Trust

    Calibrated Trust is trust in a tool that matches the tool's actual reliability, rather than being uniform across everything the tool does.

  • Structured Unevenness

    Structured Unevenness is the pattern where a model's accuracy varies by topic in the same way across vendors, rather than varying at random.
