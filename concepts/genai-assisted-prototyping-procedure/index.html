GenAI-assisted prototyping procedure

Definition

The GenAI-assisted prototyping procedure is a development workflow that combines a coding agent’s output with human review, testing, play, and handoff.

Explanation

I developed this workflow while building browser tools and games. The agent can propose a plan and produce code; I still need to decide what we are building and check whether the result does what the plan says.

StageWork to do
BriefWrite the goal, constraints, and what completion means.
PlanAsk the agent to propose decisions and give reasons for them.
ReviewRead the plan, resolve assumptions, and approve or revise it.
ReconnaissanceCheck the running project and source before changing them.
BuildImplement the change with the checks needed for its behavior.
NotebookRecord assumptions, unexpected effects, findings, and decisions that need a person.
PlaytestUse the build and compare its behavior with the plan.
DeployPublish the reviewed build and check the live version.
HandoffRecord the decisions and unresolved issues the next contributor needs.

The notebook is useful because an assumption can be wrong even when the build succeeds. Recording what we expected, what happened, and what changed gives the next contributor a clearer starting point.

Scope and evidence

This is a reported development practice from the lecture. The projects demonstrate its use, but they do not establish a comparative productivity benefit or a general learning effect.
