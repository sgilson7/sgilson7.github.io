Concept

GenAI dependence

GenAI dependence is an engagement pattern in which a student accepts a chatbot's hallucinated answer without critically evaluating it.

Also written: Dependency · Dependence · cognitive outsourcing

Defined in
Adversarial Assignment Perturbation: Effects on Help-Seeking Behaviors in Student Generative AI Chatbot Use, Sections 3.2 and 3.3
DOI
https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-29770-9_48

GenAI dependence describes what a student does when they accept the chatbot’s output without testing it.

Dependence is the other component the Independent Component Analysis separated out, and it characterises the style of information seeking. Its negative pole loads on Content_Elaboration, open-ended requests for assistance; its positive pole on Writing_Format, Writing_Instructions and Content_Example, which are rigid and highly specific requests for structure or answers.

Among the same 8 students, the lower-scoring ones — group mean perturbed assignment grade 81.3 — did not form one uniform cluster. The paper describes two distinct failing behaviours: a student who demanded formats and examples, constraining the output format while leaving the logical content alone; and a student who asked for generic advice without supplying context, and accepted advice that missed the perturbations entirely.

Two of the 8 sit outside the pattern, and the paper keeps them there. One scored highly while clustering near the low performers, because they had already solved the problem and were using the chatbot to check formatting — their low engagement was efficiency. Another scored positively on scaffolding but lacked the prior knowledge to evaluate the justification, and accepted hallucinated axioms as truth.

This is a case study of eight students in one class over one semester, and two of those eight do not fit the pattern.
