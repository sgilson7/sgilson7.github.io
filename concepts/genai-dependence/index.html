Concept

GenAI dependence

GenAI dependence is an engagement pattern in which a student accepts a chatbot's hallucinated answer without critically evaluating it.

Also written: Dependency · Dependence · cognitive outsourcing

Defined in
Adversarial Assignment Perturbation: Effects on Help-Seeking Behaviors in Student Generative AI Chatbot Use, Sections 3.2 and 3.3
DOI
https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-29770-9_48

Dependence is the other component the Independent Component Analysis separated out, and it characterises the style of information seeking rather than its depth. Its negative pole loads on Content_Elaboration, open-ended requests for assistance; its positive pole on Writing_Format, Writing_Instructions and Content_Example, which are rigid and highly specific requests for structure or answers.

Low-performing students, with a mean perturbed assignment grade of 81.3, did not form one uniform cluster. The paper describes two distinct failing behaviours: a student who demanded formats and examples, constraining the output format rather than the logical content; and a student who asked for generic advice without supplying context, and accepted advice that did not address the perturbations.

Two students do not fit the pattern, and the paper keeps them. One scored highly while clustering near the low performers, because they had already solved the problem and were using the chatbot to check formatting — low engagement as a signal of efficiency rather than passivity. Another scored positively on scaffolding but lacked the prior knowledge to evaluate the justification, and accepted hallucinated axioms as truth.
