Concept

Axiomatic Replacement

Axiomatic Replacement is a perturbation strategy that alters a question's fundamental logical premises or axioms to prevent a model retrieving a memorised proof.

Also written: axiom replacement

Defined in
Adversarial Assignment Perturbation: Effects on Help-Seeking Behaviors in Student Generative AI Chatbot Use, Table 1, step 5, and Q2
DOI
https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-29770-9_48
Tool
Perturbation Workbench

The worked example replaces standard Peano Arithmetic with a system of the same structure under an unfamiliar name, Squemp Numbers, chosen using a random, contextless word. The mathematics is the same shape; the name and the axioms are not ones the model has seen.

The rubric does the other half of the work. It imposes a 100% penalty for assuming a standard property — for instance that 1 + 1 = 2 — without deriving it. Without that penalty a model can produce the right-looking answer by reciting the familiar system, and score well for it.
