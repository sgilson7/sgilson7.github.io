Concept

Spatial Injection

Spatial Injection is a perturbation strategy that adds reasoning requiring visual or geometric interpretation to a question, so a model must track layout as well as arithmetic.

Also written: spatial constraint

Defined in
Adversarial Assignment Perturbation: Effects on Help-Seeking Behaviors in Student Generative AI Chatbot Use, Table 1, step 5, and Q1
DOI
https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-29770-9_48
Tool
Perturbation Workbench

The worked example in the paper is a counting question. It began as a rote permutation task — assigning items to slots — and gained a spatial constraint that mapped the items onto a visual grid, a salad bar at a restaurant.

What broke the model was not the grid alone but the interaction between constraints. Across multiple attempts the model failed to account for the interplay between “warm” items and “cold” slots, hallucinating formulas such as 7^6 in place of P(7, 6), and failing to satisfy the constraints simultaneously.
