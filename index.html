Sam Gilson

Sam Gilson is a computer science education researcher interested in how undergraduates use generative AI to learn: in writing proofs, in discrete mathematics, and in game AI.

Sam is a PhD student in the Game2Learn lab at North Carolina State University, advised by Dr. Tiffany Barnes. His work sits between computer science education and the practical question instructors now face every term: what students do with a chatbot when the problem in front of them is a proof.

He has taught CSC 226, Discrete Mathematics, as instructor of record, and guest-lectured on AI literacy in the Game AI course. He also builds and releases the tools his studies need, which are listed on GitHub.

Every claim on this site about a study points to the paper it came from, and nothing under review appears here. The pages below carry the DOI and the BibTeX for each item, so a citation can be taken without retyping anything.
