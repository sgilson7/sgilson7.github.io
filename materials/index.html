Course materials

These are selected materials from my teaching and research. The discrete mathematics resources come from a classroom study that used two perturbed assignments with an AI literacy session between them. The files preserve the versions distributed or submitted at that time.

CSC 226: Discrete Mathematics

AI Literacy for Mathematical Reasoning

Lecture · PDF · 18 pages · Summer 2026

The lecture introduces ways to examine chatbot-generated mathematical reasoning. It presents three procedures: checking a proof, strengthening a prompt, and working through a problem with a chatbot.

Reading these materials

The original lecture uses simplified explanations of LLM training. For a more careful account, see the revised web lecture: next-token generation describes a decoding process, and post-training can change performance as well as presentation. Neither a confident explanation nor a successful small-case check establishes that a general proof is valid.

The procedures were taught as part of an ongoing study. Their presence in these materials does not establish that they improve learning. See the preprint for the study’s status and evidence.
