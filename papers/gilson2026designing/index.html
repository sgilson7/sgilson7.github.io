Designing AI-Resistant Assignments via Iterative Perturbation to Promote Interactive Learning

Published poster

Sam Gilson

Proceedings of the 57th ACM Technical Symposium on Computer Science Education V.2 (SIGCSE TS 2026), p. 1745

Citation year: 2026.

Publisher record

Summary

This poster presents iterative perturbation as an approach to assignment design: test a question with a target chatbot, revise the question, and evaluate the new response against an explicit rubric. The design challenge is to make the chatbot’s limitations observable while keeping the mathematical task appropriate for students.

What this study can tell us

This is a poster record. The summary describes the design approach; it is not a claim that the poster established an improvement in student learning.

Concepts used in this work

Iterative perturbation process · One-Shot GenAI Resistance
